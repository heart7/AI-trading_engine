# PROTECTION_UNVERIFIED

Class and severity: S1

**What fired.** A position's venue-resident stop could not be read back within tolerance.

**What the system already did.** Entries blocked on the venue; the verifier retries; after the deadline the position is reduced (§8.3).

**What you own.** Log in to the venue and check the stop by hand. Do not place orders by hand unless the stop is missing.

**Resolve when.** Read-back matches; rationale recorded. Incidents are never dismissed; resolving one needs a written rationale (§7.9).

# NO_ON_CALL

Class and severity: H2

**What fired.** Nobody is on call on a day the engine is at CANARY or LIVE.

**What the system already did.** Engine in STOP: entries blocked; venue-resident stops keep protecting positions.

**What you own.** Add a shift to policy/oncall.yaml or accept STOP for the holiday.

**Resolve when.** Rota covers the day. Incidents are never dismissed; resolving one needs a written rationale (§7.9).

# GEO_EGRESS_MISMATCH

Class and severity: S1

**What fired.** Traffic left from a location other than your declared residence (INV-43).

**What the system already did.** Everything to that venue blocked. VPNs and location masking are forbidden.

**What you own.** Turn off any VPN or proxy on the host. Do not resume until egress matches GB.

**Resolve when.** Egress verified as GB. Incidents are never dismissed; resolving one needs a written rationale (§7.9).

# STABLECOIN_DEPEG

Class and severity: S2

**What fired.** A stablecoin left its par band.

**What the system already did.** That stablecoin is reduce-only; valuations use its market price.

**What you own.** Decide whether to convert balances to USD at the venue.

**Resolve when.** Back in band or balances converted. Incidents are never dismissed; resolving one needs a written rationale (§7.9).
